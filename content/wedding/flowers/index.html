We love Lego Flowers!

We wanted to share our love of Lego with you. You should find a bag and a vase at your seat. Inside the bag is a Lego flower you can build.

How to

  1. Find your set on this page.
    Follow the link in the Table of Contents that matches the set number on the note in your bag.

  2. Find your flower in the list.
    Flowers are listed with a hint to help identify them. Follow the link to the individual instructions for your flower. This will open a PDF from the Lego website and scroll to the correct page.

  3. Build your flower!
    You can follow the instructions exactly, or you can make it your own.

  4. Put your flower in the tiny vase.
    It is yours to keep.
